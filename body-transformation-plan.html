Tea and supplement rules

  • Two or three teas are fine; three are included above.
  • Cap each at 4 g sugar and 60 mL toned milk.
  • One protein scoop is sufficient on this menu.
  • Use two scoops only if eggs or one paneer serving is omitted.
  • No fat burners or detox products.

Water, salt and digestion

  • Drink 3.0–3.5 L fluid, plus more when sweating heavily.
  • Keep salt quantity similar from day to day.
  • Walk for 10 minutes after both major meals.
  • Test onion or large dairy portions individually if bloated.
  • Avoid fizzy drinks, sugar alcohols and restaurant meals near the shoot.

Execution

  • Compounds: rest 2–3 minutes.
  • Isolation work: rest 60–90 seconds.
  • Keep 1–3 good repetitions in reserve.
  • Add load only after all sets reach the top of the range.
  • Do weights before cardio.

Cardio and steps

  • Incline walk near 5–8% and 4.5–5.5 km/h, adjusted to fitness.
  • Use a pace where short sentences remain possible.
  • Target 10,000 total steps, including treadmill steps.
  • If currently below 5,000, use 8,000 for days 1–3 first.

Final 48 hours

Keep

  • 3.0–3.5 L normal daily fluid
  • Stable salt intake
  • Familiar protein sources
  • Approximately 170–210 g carbohydrates
  • Eight hours sleep

Reduce or avoid

  • Alcohol and restaurant meals
  • Carbonated drinks and sugar alcohols
  • Beans, giant salads and excess onion
  • Unfamiliar foods or supplements
  • Hard training that creates soreness
Optional low-residue adjustment In the final 24 hours only, reduce fibre to about 15–20 g if you already tested this successfully. Choose rice, peeled potato, banana, yogurt, eggs and protein as tolerated. Never eliminate water or sodium.

Feedback loop

Use trends, not emotion.

Daily body weight is useful data; a single daily change is not a verdict.

Measurements

  • Weigh daily after waking, bathroom, and before food or fluid.
  • Compare rolling averages, not isolated readings.
  • Measure waist on preparation days 1, 8, 15 and 21.
  • Measure at the navel, relaxed after a normal exhale.
  • Take three readings and record the middle one.

Progress photos

  • Take photos on days 1, 8, 15 and shoot morning.
  • Keep room, lighting, distance, lens and time consistent.
  • Use front, side and back views.
  • Capture one relaxed and one lightly flexed set.
  • Do not use a workout pump for tracking photos.
